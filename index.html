🌡️ Drying Protocols by Product
Product TypeTempDurationTarget
Ragi Noodles55°C6–8 hrsSnap clean, 10–12% moisture

Critical: Snap Test

Noodle must snap cleanly without bending. No flexibility = properly dried. Test before packing.

Beetroot Warning

Die temperature MAX 88–92°C. Never exceed. Blanching destroys colour. Citric acid must dissolve first.

⚙️ Production Workflow
  • 1
    Soak Isabgol
    10g + 50ml warm water · rest 15 min until gel forms
  • 2
    Prepare Water Blend
    RO 70% + Tap 30% · heat to specified temp
  • 3
    Dry Mix
    Weigh all dry ingredients · mix thoroughly before adding liquid
  • 4
    Extrusion
    Feed slowly · monitor die temp · check strand quality
  • 5
    Drying
    Spread on trays · maintain temperature · snap test before pulling
  • 6
    Cool & Pack
    Cool completely (30 min) · add masala packet · seal · check seal quality
